JK_Pump_Build — Standing Requirements
=====================================

Authoritative detailed instruction:
  Projects/JK_Pump_Build/Agent_do_pump.md

Source data:
  Projects/JK_Pump_Build/Pump_details.xlsx

Structural reference only:
  Projects/JK_Pump_Build/Horizontal_Pump_Example.txt

The structural reference may be used only to understand E3D hierarchy,
component relationships and modeling approach. Its dimensions, coordinates,
positions, orientations, proportions and equipment parameters must not be
copied.

Project requirements
--------------------

1. Generate one independent AVEVA E3D PML model/script per Excel pump tag.
2. Excel coordinates are the geometric centre of the underside of the
   rectangular baseplate. They are not the casing centre, top face or a nozzle
   datum.
3. Excel Length and Width are baseplate dimensions only and must not be changed.
4. The suction direction defines the pump longitudinal arrangement:
   suction -> casing -> motor. The motor is at the opposite end.
5. The discharge direction does not override the suction-defined longitudinal
   direction.
6. Use each tag's top-view PNG as the primary configuration reference where
   available. Missing detail may use documented engineering Reference Values,
   but every assumed value must be listed in the deliverable.
7. User/Excel values outrank every project default or engineering assumption.
8. Generate PML1 only. Follow SKILL.md, the Iron Rules and the shared
   PMLBuilder validation rules. For every oriented primitive, emit ORI before
   POS; treat POS as the primitive geometric centre.
9. Verify baseplate length, width, thickness, underside-centre coordinate,
   elevation and suction-defined orientation numerically for every pump.
10. Each pump shall occupy one independent E3D `EQUI` (EQUIPMENT) hierarchy.
    Each individual macro shall contain exactly one `NEW EQUI`, named from that
    pump's tag with E3D-safe underscores.
11. Deliver an individual macro and verification record for each tag, plus an
    uncertainty/exception report. Do not report E3D execution as verified until
    the user has loaded the macro successfully.

Coordinate convention established from the project
--------------------------------------------------

- Units: millimetres.
- World axes: E = East, N = North, U = Up.
- Positive U is upward.
- Excel U is the underside elevation of the baseplate.
- Local positive longitudinal direction is from motor toward the pump casing.
  It normally equals suction direction, except for explicitly documented
  side-suction special pumps.

Client-requested high-detail pass — 2026-07-29
-----------------------------------------------

- Refine every pump as an individual engineering model; do not apply one
  undifferentiated exterior to the full register.
- Interpret the supplied service name and available tag-specific top view before
  selecting the casing, bearing-frame, driver and auxiliary construction.
- Preserve all underlying project parameters without modification: tag,
  coordinate, baseplate length/width/thickness, longitudinal axis, suction
  direction and discharge direction.
- Add realistic visible construction within the confirmed baseplate envelope:
  welded rails/cross-members, exposed base/driver fasteners, casing split flange
  and flange-face fasteners, bearing/seal housing, flexible coupling and guard,
  finned TEFC motor, motor feet, fan grille, terminal box, cable gland, lifting
  lug and nameplate.
- Differentiate dilution, filtrate, screen/stock feed, booster, wire-wash,
  washer-feed, MC and scrubber-circulation duties. Matched duty/size pump pairs
  may retain equivalent construction where the supplied data supports no real
  difference.
- MC pumps shall retain tag-specific separation/degassing and auxiliary-drive
  layouts from their top-view drawings; missing views remain UNCONFIRMED.
- Thread pitch, exact hex flats, motor manufacturer, bolt grade, materials and
  vendor dimensions are not supplied. Model bounded visual envelopes as
  Reference Values and do not claim them as confirmed fabrication data.

Client correction to the high-detail pass — 2026-07-29
-------------------------------------------------------

- The previous driver envelope was visibly undersized. Use a substantial,
  skid-scaled TEFC/MC motor envelope and reject motors below the documented
  length, height and length-to-finned-diameter proportion floors.
- Motor cooling ribs shall be axial ribs distributed around the complete 360°
  stator circumference. A few top/side BOX strips are not acceptable. Every rib
  must overlap the stator solid and project beyond it.
- Model an open fan-grille lattice, a separate mounting pad and
  barrel-connected foot web; do not use a solid grille disc or detached feet.
- Preserve positive, non-overlapping axial interfaces from motor through
  coupling and bearing frame to casing. Every motor pad and hold-down bolt must
  bear on a skid longitudinal rail in plan, not only touch it in elevation.
- Heterogeneity must be physical, not a unique code or comment. Motor family,
  casing family, coupling guard, duty feature and MC package selections must
  alter generated geometry. Identical documented pump pairs shall remain the
  same family rather than receiving invented mirror/details.
- The 27 plan images support family/outline and clearly visible accessory
  differences, not exact fin/bolt counts. Such counts remain S/M/L/XL
  engineering Reference Values. `203-PU-020`, `205-PU-004` and `205-PU-032`
  remain explicitly UNCONFIRMED because their tag views are missing.
- Preserve the 30 independent one-EQUI-per-pump macros, and additionally
  deliver one directly generated all-pumps PML file for one-click loading. The
  combined file shall contain 30 independent `EQUI` elements, not one common
  equipment hierarchy, and must pass the same numerical checks pump by pump.
