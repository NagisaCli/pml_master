## Data-quality exceptions

These are reported explicitly. Source measurements are not overwritten;
structural adaptations are visible as Reference Values or conflicts.

1. **`204-PU-028` is out of scope.** It carries a casing row (615 x 140) and an MC casing offset (920 mm), but it does not appear in `location_nozzle_reference.txt` (the project equipment set) or in the engine/shaft CSV. Its service name, *POST OXYGEN WASHER DISCHARGE MC PUMP*, matches no project tag, so it is not a typo for one. Not modelled.
2. **Three tags lack a motor/shaft CSV row.** `203-PU-020` uses `203-PU-031`; `203-PU-026` uses `205-PU-024`; `205-PU-004` uses `204-PU-006`. The nearest measured non-MC family is selected by plate length/width and casing diameter, with an axial-occupancy penalty that rejects donors requiring excessive shaft compression. Axial values scale with plate length and radial values with plate width. These are Reference Values, not claimed measurements.
3. **The three MC rows have shaft fields `N/A`.** No free `DRIVE_SHAFT` is invented. A structurally continuous close-coupled lantern spans the motor-to-casing gap.
4. **Every motor now requires two similar end frustums.** If one end length is zero/N/A, the measured opposite end supplies the common length. Both cones taper from the main stator to an outward face at exactly 2/3 of main diameter. This user instruction overrides conflicting rear/front diameter fields.
5. **`205-PU-012` was visibly too short in r7.** r7 compressed its main barrel to 717.0 mm inside the 997.4 mm source total. r8 retains 997.4 mm as the main barrel, then adds the 129.4 mm rear small barrel and two 75.5 mm cones outside it, giving a 1277.8 mm motor envelope. Its free shaft is refitted to the new motor front without changing source diameters or part ratios.
6. **r8 still terminated 27 non-MC motor tails with a CYLINDER.** r9 removes the exterior rear-small cylinder and absorbs its axial allowance into the main stator, so the rear CONE is the physical tail on all 30 pumps without shortening any motor envelope. On `205-PU-012`, the main barrel therefore becomes 1126.8 mm while the corrected total remains 1277.8 mm.
7. **MC and zero-side component totals are resolved inside the motor envelope.** The main barrel absorbs the length correction after both required cones are inserted; each conflict remains flagged.
8. **Free-shaft source offsets do not always close both interfaces.** For each non-MC pump, the three source part ratios are uniformly fitted from the motor front face to controlled casing engagement. Source diameters and the two-CYLINDER/one-CONE topology are unchanged; structure therefore takes priority over a detached literal placement.
9. **`205-PU-046`, `205-PU-047`, `205-PU-048`** have motor/shaft data but no casing row, and no same-named casing twin to borrow from. Their casing sizes are DERIVED from the baseplate and flagged.
10. **Casing/accepted driver envelope vs suction elevation conflict.** For the pumps marked *raised* above, the casing diameter in `casing_reference.txt` or the accepted r6 driver clearance envelope is larger than twice the available elevation, so a machine centred on the suction axis would pass through the baseplate and below the foundation. The shaft/support elevation remains the accepted r6 value; r7 does not reintroduce a DISH. Nozzle plan positions and directions remain untouched.
11. **Twenty-eight pumps have an upward discharge.** r12 divides them by the verified catalogue DN versus casing axial width. Fourteen nozzles within that width use no additive connector: the native NOZZLE root penetrates the crown and 75% remains outside, providing margin over the required 70%. Fourteen overwidth DN150 openings use a 90%-of-width lower CYLINDER socket and one concentric CONE. The CONE upper face equals the native NOZZLE bottom; there is no added upper neck. These are EQUI primitives, not PIPE/BRANCH items.
12. **`205-PU-054` has a fixed 308 mm plan offset between its MC casing centre and fixed up-nozzle centre.** Neither source position is moved. A casing-owned horizontal CYLINDER basic body overlaps both the volute and the vertical lower socket so the added construction remains one solid chain. This is flagged as a Reference Value pending E3D review.
