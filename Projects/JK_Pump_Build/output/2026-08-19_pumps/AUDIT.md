## Data-quality exceptions

These are reported, not silently corrected, so the client can fix the
source data rather than inheriting a guess.

1. **`204-PU-028` is out of scope.** It carries a casing row (615 x 140) and an MC casing offset (920 mm), but it does not appear in `location_nozzle_reference.txt` (the project equipment set) or in the engine/shaft CSV. Its service name, *POST OXYGEN WASHER DISCHARGE MC PUMP*, matches no project tag, so it is not a typo for one. Not modelled.
2. **`203-PU-020` and `205-PU-004`** appear only in the equipment set: no casing row and no motor/shaft record. Only the baseplate, position and the two nozzles are modelled; the casing is derived from the plate and the driver is omitted entirely rather than invented.
3. **`203-PU-026`** has a casing row but no motor/shaft record; its driver and shaft are not modelled.
4. **`205-PU-046`, `205-PU-047`, `205-PU-048`** have motor/shaft data but no casing row, and no same-named casing twin to borrow from. Their casing sizes are DERIVED from the baseplate and flagged.
5. **Casing radius vs suction elevation conflict.** For the pumps marked *raised* above, the casing diameter in `casing_reference.txt` is larger than twice the suction nozzle elevation, so a volute centred on the suction axis would pass through the baseplate and below the foundation. The two figures come from different sources and cannot both be honoured. The shaft was raised to the minimum height that seats the machine on the plate; nozzle plan positions and directions are untouched.
